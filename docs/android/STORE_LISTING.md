# Zing: Google Play store listing

Copy to paste into Play Console. Everything here describes what the app in this pull request really does.

**App name (30):** Zing: Notes, Cards & Quizzes

**Short description (80):** Turn your notes into flashcards and quizzes. Study offline, with your school.

**Full description:**

Zing is a study app for high-school students. Keep your notes in notebooks, turn them into flashcards and quizzes,
and find anything you've written in seconds.

NOTEBOOKS
• Make a notebook for each subject, with sub-books for topics
• Write notes that save as you type, even with no internet
• Pin, archive, reorder and search them

FIND IT FAST
• Type a topic and Zing shows the notes and the exact passages that mention it, grouped by notebook
• Zing only answers from your own notes. If it isn't in your notes, it tells you

CARDS AND QUIZZES
• Flashcards that come back just before you'd forget them
• Quizzes that explain every answer
• A Path that takes a notebook level by level

MAGIC IMPORT
• Bring in a PDF, Word or PowerPoint file, a photo of your notes, a recording, or pasted text
• Quizlet, Anki and spreadsheet exports become cards straight away
• You check every card before it is saved

STUDY WITH YOUR SCHOOL
• Weekly leagues and school leaderboards
• Share a deck with a link

MAKE IT YOURS
• Five themes, light and dark, your own accent colour and text size
• Works on phones and tablets

Your notebooks sync with your Zing account, so they are also on the Zing website.

Zing AI (optional) is for students aged 14 and over with a Google or email sign-in, and has daily limits. AI can make
mistakes: always check the cards and answers it gives.

Zing is free. Zing Plus is an optional subscription.
