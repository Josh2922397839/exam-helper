Using the app

Terms of Use

Zing (formerly Exam Helper) offers study guides, practice activities, your own decks with Magic import, school leaderboards, leagues, Live games, challenges and study groups. These terms describe the intended use of those features. The Google sign-in screen may still show the earlier name, ExamForge.

Effective date: 7 November 2026.

1. Use it as a study aid

Use the app alongside your course materials and your teacher’s instructions. Study content, answer explanations, student-made decks and cards written by Zing AI can contain mistakes or become out of date. Check AI-made cards before you rely on them. Check important points against your current syllabus and reliable sources, and report errors using the contact below. App scores, XP and league positions measure activity in the app; they are not official school grades or a prediction of an exam result.

2. Keep participation respectful

  • Use your real first and last name when you set up, a suitable username, and treat other learners respectfully. Do not impersonate another person or school.
  • Only challenge, follow or add people to study groups in a friendly way. Do not use challenges, group names or shared sets to bully, spam or embarrass anyone.
  • Import, share and photograph notes and images only when you have permission to use them, and only study material. Do not upload or share private personal information, photos of people, account-transfer codes or content that harasses other people.
  • Do not use scripts, false scores or other attempts to manipulate league rankings, disrupt live games, or interfere with other people’s accounts or the service.
  • Follow the rules your school or examiner sets for assessments. Practice tools do not grant permission to use them during a test.

3. Your decks, sharing and games

Decks you create (cards, notes and subdecks) are saved in your browser and may be included in cloud backup when you use an online account. Sharing a deck by link or listing it in a school library lets other people read and save its cards. Coins for saves are only for decks you made; a deck you saved from someone else shares their original link. Share links and game codes can be forwarded. Removing your own set or ending a game cannot recall copies or information other people already received.

A custom photo or GIF is kept on your device; other people see your selected built-in avatar. See the Privacy Policy for the distinction between local content, cloud saves and publicly visible information.

4. Zing AI, coins, power-ups and age

Zing AI is for students 14 and older who sign in with Google or a confirmed email. It has daily limits and can be unavailable or switched off at any time; quick cards and manual cards still work. Coins and power-ups are just for fun inside the app. They have no money value, cannot be bought or sold, and may be reset or changed. Students under 13 can use Zing, but friend finding, name display and study groups are switched off for them, as described in the Privacy Policy.

5. Look after account access

Guest-account access depends on the browser’s saved sign-in. Clearing site data or losing the device can remove that access. Use the available sign-in connection or account-transfer feature when moving to another device, and keep transfer codes private. Account and Settings let you edit your profile, sign out or delete your account; their effects are described in the privacy policy.

6. Availability and external resources

Online features depend on an internet connection and external services. An interruption can delay sign-in, cloud saves, school search or live games. Offline access depends on what the browser has cached. Linked learning resources are provided by their respective websites and may change independently of Zing.
