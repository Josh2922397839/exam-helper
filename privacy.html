Privacy Policy

Exam Helper provides study guides, quizzes, flashcards, study sets and optional online leagues and games. This policy explains what is saved on your device, what goes online, and the controls available to you.

The Google sign-in configuration for Exam Helper uses the name ExamForge. Questions about this app or your information can be sent to 06joshuaandersone4798@gmail.com.

Effective date: 6 November 2026.

1. Studying on your device

The app uses your browser’s local storage for study progress and settings. This includes topics visited, quiz results and mistakes, flashcard review levels, practice activity, XP and streaks, study sets you create, and preferences such as theme and grade selection. Browser storage also keeps sign-in sessions and some account and interface settings. Temporary information, such as a live-game participant ID or a pending sign-in, can be held in session storage.

A service worker caches app files and study resources so previously loaded parts of the site can work offline. Device data belongs to that browser and website address; another browser or website address does not automatically have the same saved data.

2. Online accounts and cloud saves

Online features use Supabase for authentication, database storage and live-game connections. Joining a league with a nickname creates a guest authentication account. The league profile stores your nickname, selected school, grade and built-in avatar, together with league and XP activity. The online system also stores following relationships, school-library sets you publish, saved-set activity, and account-transfer and request-limit records.

When cloud backup is used, the app sends a copy of your study-progress state and your locally created study sets to your account. This supports restoring or combining progress on another device. Study notes that you put into a set are part of that backup, even when you have not published the set in a school library.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in when it is available, Google supplies basic identity information to Supabase Auth, such as your account identifier, name, email address and profile information. The Google sign-in configuration is limited to basic identity permissions: openid, email and profile. They are used to sign you in or connect Google sign-in to your existing app account. The app does not request permission to read Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars.

Your Google name and email are not fields in the app’s public league profile. Your league nickname is chosen separately. The app uses the sign-in session to identify your account and retrieve its profile and saved study data. You can also review or remove the Google connection in your Google Account connections; doing so does not itself remove data already stored in Exam Helper.

3. What other people can see

  • Profiles and leaderboards: your nickname, selected avatar, grade, school, league, XP, ranks, and follower/following counts can appear to other visitors. Choose a nickname you are comfortable sharing.
  • School libraries: publishing a study set makes its title and card content available with its author nickname and school. School-library content can be browsed by other visitors; it is not a private classroom folder.
  • Share links: a shared-set link contains the set’s text inside the link. Challenge links can contain a nickname, subject and score. Anyone who receives a link can use it and forward it. Removing your local copy cannot recall a link or copies that others already saved.
  • Live games: participants use a game code to connect through Supabase Realtime. Participants exchange nicknames, built-in avatars, presence, game questions, selected answers and response times, scores and reactions. Anyone with the game code can try to join. The host’s device runs the game; leaving disconnects that browser from the channel.

Keep private information out of public nicknames, school-library sets, game names and shared links.

4. Profile pictures and GIFs

A custom photo or animated GIF you select is processed in your browser and saved on that device. It is not included in the app’s cloud progress/set backup and is not sent as your public league picture. Other people see your selected built-in avatar. You can remove the custom picture in the picture editor or clear the site’s browser data.

5. External services

EdgeOne hosts and delivers the website. The app also loads Google Fonts and some software, icons or 3D components through Google Fonts, cdnjs/Cloudflare and jsDelivr. These services receive the network requests needed to provide those resources, which can include connection information such as an IP address and browser/request details.

School searches use the app’s Supabase school directory. Choosing Search the world map sends the school-name query and selected country to the Overpass API for an OpenStreetMap lookup. Choosing to add a school submits the school name, country and any region information you enter; map-based records may include the school’s map location. This lookup does not request your device’s location.

The app also links to educational reference websites. Opening those links takes you to the relevant website, which handles your visit under its own policies.

6. Your controls and deletion

  • Edit your account: change your league nickname, school, grade and avatar in Account.
  • Remove a published set: use the set’s school-library removal action. Copies already saved by other people may remain with them.
  • Sign out: signs you out in this browser and can also end sign-in sessions for the same account on other devices. It keeps local study progress, sets and your custom picture.
  • Reset progress: resets the app’s study-progress state. It does not by itself remove your sets, custom picture or authentication account.
  • Clear site data: your browser’s site-data controls can remove local storage and cached files. This can also remove access to a guest account on that device. Clearing browser data does not delete the cloud account.
  • Delete your league account: the Account action removes the app’s league profile, cloud save, following relationships, published sets, saved-set records and transfer-code record. It does not clear device data, remove contributed school-directory entries, or automatically delete the underlying Supabase Auth sign-in record.

For questions about information associated with your sign-in, removal requests, or a problem using these controls, contact 06joshuaandersone4798@gmail.com. Keep account-transfer codes private: possession of a valid code can move the account.

Device information remains until the applicable in-app control removes it or your browser clears it. Cloud data is separate from browser storage and is affected by the cloud deletion controls described above. This policy does not specify a fixed retention period for hosting, authentication or service logs, or deletion of third-party backups.
