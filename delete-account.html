Delete your Zing account

You can delete your Zing account and the information stored with it at any time, from the app or from this website. This page explains how, what is removed and what is kept.

1. How to delete your account

In the Android app

  1. Open Me, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete everything.

On this website

  1. Sign in at exam-helper.edgeone.app.
  2. Open Settings, then Delete account, and confirm.

If you can't sign in

Use the contact in the Privacy Policy and say which sign-in (Google account or email address) or username the account uses. We may ask you to show the account is yours before deleting it. Never send a password or an account-transfer code.

2. What is deleted

  • Your profile: username, name, age, school year, school, avatar, league, XP and ranks.
  • Your cloud backup: study progress, your decks, cards and notes.
  • Your friends list (who you follow and who follows you), blocks, challenges and study-group memberships and messages.
  • Decks you shared by link or in a school library.
  • Your appearance settings and notebook details kept with your account.
  • Your Zing Plus entitlement, if you had one.
  • Your sign-in record (the link between Zing and your Google account or email address). It is removed when you delete from the Android app. If you deleted on the website and want this record removed as well, use the contact above.

Deleting from the Android app also clears Zing's data from that phone. On the website, use your browser's clear site data to remove what is saved in that browser.

3. What is kept, and why

  • Copies other students already saved. If someone added your shared deck to their own decks, their copy stays with them.
  • Schools you added to the directory. A school's name and place stay so other students at that school can find it. They are no longer linked to you.
  • Reports you made. A report about a profile, a deck or an AI answer stays so it can still be reviewed. Your name is removed from it.
  • Purchase records. If you subscribed to Zing Plus, a scrambled fingerprint of the Google Play purchase is kept, without any link to you, so that refunds and chargebacks from Google can still be handled. Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under your Google account.
  • Service logs and backups held by our hosting providers follow those providers' own schedules.

4. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription

Zing Plus is billed by Google Play. Cancel it in the Play Store (Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions) before or after deleting your account, or Google will keep charging you.

5. Delete some things without deleting your account

  • A shared deck: open the deck and choose to stop sharing it.
  • A notebook, note or card: delete it in the app; it is removed from your cloud backup the next time the app backs up.
  • Study progress: use Reset progress on the website.
